Statistical modeling of extreme rainfall events (Cévenol episodes) for natural catastrophe risk assessment. This project was carried out during my research position at CCR (Caisse Centrale de Réassurance) and focuses on the analysis of extreme weather events using Extreme Value Theory (EVT). The study relies on DRIAS climate projections based on the ALADIN numerical model developed by Météo-France and CNRM. The methodology includes the Block Maxima approach with the Generalized Extreme Value (GEV) distribution and the Peaks-Over-Threshold (POT) approach with the Generalized Pareto Distribution (GPD) to estimate the occurrence and intensity of extreme precipitation events.
